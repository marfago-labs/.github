# marfago labs

**My lab for building AI systems that understand research text.**

I'm **Marco Fago** ([@marfago](https://github.com/marfago)) — an **AI software engineer** in Dublin. This is where I run experiments and ship tools: not a grab bag of repos, but one connected body of work around **research intelligence** — how we ingest long-form knowledge, compress and summarize it faithfully, structure and retrieve it, and **measure** whether the machinery actually works.

---

## What I believe

- **Quality is engineering.** LLM demos are easy; reliable pipelines — with tests, metrics, and clear failure modes — are the job.
- **Measure before you trust.** Compression, NER, retrieval: I score them with benchmarks and gold data, not vibes.
- **Agents are glue, not magic.** They orchestrate steps; the design is plans, validation, retries, and observability.
- **Research text deserves its own stack.** arXiv papers and YouTube talks are not generic chat input; they need dedicated ingest and evaluation.

---

## What I build here

**Turn firehoses into usable signal.** Ingest and normalize arXiv and YouTube; compress long text while preserving entities and numbers; evaluate with semantic overlap and faithfulness checks.

**Build ground truth, not just models.** Curate and generate NER gold — manual annotation, multi-sample LLM consensus, and synthetic corpora with entity-first planning, exactly-once span validation, and mixed genres (papers, news, blogs, transcripts).

**Compare backends honestly.** Benchmark pattern, transformer, and zero-shot NER on the same gold so choices are data-driven.

**Ship tools I actually use.** ArticleRecommender — discovery, chat, and archive for the papers and channels I follow — is the product-shaped direction of the same stack.

Active lab work includes [text-compressor](https://github.com/marfago-labs/text-compressor), [ner-gold-generator](https://github.com/marfago-labs/ner-gold-generator), and [ner-detector](https://github.com/marfago-labs/ner-detector).

---

## How it fits together

One through-line: **raw research text → trustworthy compression and structure → embeddings and discovery → evaluated extraction.** Each project in the lab is a layer of that stack, built to compose.
